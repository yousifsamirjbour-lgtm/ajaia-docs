SCENE 7: SCOPE CUTS & PRAGMATIC TRADEOFFS (02:15 - 02:35 | 600 frames)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visual:
- Engineering tradeoff comparison table:
  * Header Auth (X-User-Email): Demo-friendly, zero friction, spoofable in prod
  * Concurrency: Last-write-wins chosen over complex CRDTs for simplicity
  * Storage: Standard HTML chosen over proprietary AST for open portability
  * SQLite: Local zero-setup persistence; volume mount recommended for cloud

Presenter Narration:
"To deliver an airtight solution within the assignment timeline, deliberate
tradeoffs were made. Header-based authentication was selected to enable instant
role-switching without login barriers, though a production deployment would use
JWT Bearer tokens. We prioritized a deterministic last-write-wins concurrency model
and portable HTML storage over heavier CRDT abstractions."
